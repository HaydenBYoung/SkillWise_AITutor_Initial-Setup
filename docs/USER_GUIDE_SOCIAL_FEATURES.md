# User Guide: Leaderboard & Peer Review

## 🏆 How to Get on the Leaderboard

### Step 1: Complete Challenges

1. Go to **Challenges** page (🚀 in sidebar)
2. Click on any challenge to view details
3. Submit your work in the submission form:
   - Enter your code/solution
   - Add an explanation (optional)
   - Click "Submit Work"
4. You'll get AI feedback immediately
5. **NEW!** Click the "Mark as Complete" button to earn points
   - Easy challenge: **10 points**
   - Medium challenge: **25 points**
   - Hard challenge: **50 points**

### Step 2: Complete Goals

1. Go to **Goals** page (🎯 in sidebar)
2. Click on a goal you're working on
3. **NEW!** Click the "Mark Goal as Complete" button
   - Earn **100+ points** based on completion percentage

### Step 3: View the Leaderboard

1. Go to **Leaderboard** page (🏆 in sidebar)
2. See your rank, points, and level
3. Filter by timeframe: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, All-Time
4. See where you stand among other learners!

## 👥 How Peer Review Works

### Getting Work Reviewed

**When you submit work for a challenge, peer reviewers are automatically assigned!**

1. Go to **Challenges** page
2. Click on any challenge
3. Submit your work (code, text, or link)
4. **2-3 peer reviewers are automatically assigned** based on:
   - Similar skill level (within ±2 levels of you)
   - Availability (not overloaded with reviews)
   - Variety (different people review your work)

That's it! You don't need to do anything else. The system handles reviewer assignment automatically.

### Reviewing Others' Work

1. Go to **Peer Review** page (👥 in sidebar)
2. Click the **"Pending"** tab
3. You'll see submissions assigned to you for review
4. Click "Review" on any submission
5. Provide feedback:
   - Give a 1-5 star rating
   - Rate on specific criteria (clarity, completeness, code quality, creativity)
   - Write at least 50 characters of constructive feedback
6. Click "Submit Review"
7. **You earn 15+ points** for reviewing!
8. The author earns **5 points** for receiving your review

### Viewing Feedback

**Received Tab**: See all reviews you've received on your work

- View ratings and detailed feedback
- Learn from peer suggestions
- Track which submissions got the best reviews

**Given Tab**: See all reviews you've submitted

- Track your reviewing history
- See how helpful you've been to others

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Q: I submitted work but don't see it in Peer Review**

- Check that you clicked "Submit Work" (not just typed it)
- Refresh the page
- Check the browser console for errors

**Q: Leaderboard won't load**

- Make sure you're logged in
- Check that the backend is running
- Refresh the page
- Check browser console for errors

**Q: I don't have any pending reviews**

- Not everyone gets assigned reviews immediately
- Need more users submitting work to get review assignments
- Check back later as more submissions come in

**Q: Can't mark challenge as complete**

- You need to submit work first
- Can only mark each challenge complete once
- If already completed, button will be disabled

**Q: My rank isn't updating**

- Rankings update when you earn points
- May take a few seconds to refresh
- Try switching timeframe filters and back
- Refresh the page
